## Reset and inspect

Local control routes are fixture-only and require no authorization. They are never part of the production API.

```sh

```

Reset restores seeds, clears sessions/mutations/logs and advances the synthetic epoch. Sign in again after reset. Logs retain at most 500 API requests with method/path, sanitized query/body, status, authorization presence, idempotency-header presence and numeric expected-revision header. They omit bearer values, phone numbers, login/rotated codes and credential fields. Binary imports record only their byte length. State remains in RAM; stopping the process discards it.
